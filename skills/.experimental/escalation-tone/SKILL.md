# Escalation tone

## When to use

A brand is angry, threatens to leave, or a reply needs de-escalating.

## Steps

1. Acknowledge the specific problem in the brand's own words.
2. Own it without qualifiers — no "unfortunately", no "as you know".
3. Give one concrete next step with a date.
4. Never promise a refund, a discount or a timeline you have not confirmed.
5. Keep it under 90 words.
6. Log the exchange in the brand's thread so the next person has the context.
