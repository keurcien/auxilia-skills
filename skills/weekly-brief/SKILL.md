# Weekly brief

## When to use

The user asks for the weekly brief, the Monday recap, or "how did last week go".

## Steps

1. Pull last week's GMV, order count and the top three operations by GMV.
2. Open with the GMV figure and its change versus the previous week.
3. List the top three operations, one line each: name, GMV, what drove it.
4. Close with exactly one risk line — the thing most likely to hurt next week.
5. Keep the whole brief under 150 words. No headers, no bullet nesting.
6. End with the date range covered.
