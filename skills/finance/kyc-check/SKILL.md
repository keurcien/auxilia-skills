# KYC check

## When to use

A new brand is about to run its first operation, or someone asks whether a
brand has been vetted.

## Steps

1. Confirm the legal entity name, registration number and country.
2. Check the registration number against the national register (ask the user
   for the lookup result if you cannot reach it).
3. Verify the bank account holder matches the legal entity.
4. Record the three checks and their outcome in one short table.
5. If any check fails, stop and say which one — do not continue onboarding.
